# Audio script template

A five-minute spoken overview of the day's brief. Write it after the page is finished, from the page only.

## Rules

- Length: 700 to 750 words. At speed 0.95 that lands near 4:35.
- Write for the ear: short sentences, one idea each.
- Say numbers the way a person would: "sixty-three billion dollars", "forty-five percent", "November ninth".
- Spell out acronyms or say them as words a listener knows. "Initial public offering", not "IPO".
- No em dashes or en dashes. No sentences starting with "It". No lists, headers, or markdown.
- No facts that are not on the page.
- Plain text only. Save as `script.txt` and run `templates/scripts/make_audio.sh script.txt`.

## Outline

1. **Intro** (about 30 words): "This is <BRIEF NAME>, for <Weekday, Month D, YYYY>. Five minutes, public sources only."
2. **Key numbers** (about 90 words): each figure, then one short sentence on what it is.
3. **The Big Three** (about 330 words): for each story, the headline, what happened, why it matters, and the call with its confidence.
4. **The quadrants** (about 150 words): one beat each for the known unknowns (the biggest open question and its date), the unknown knowns (one ignored fact), and the unknown unknowns (one outside surprise).
5. **Optional: what this means for <YOUR COMPANY>** (about 70 words, only if the page has the optional module): the top opportunity and the top risk, framed as an outside read from public sources. Without the module, spend these words on one or two more confirmed stories from the page.
6. **Thesis status** (about 60 words): Unchanged, Revised, or Rewritten, the three parts in one sentence each, and whether any falsifier happened.
7. **Sign-off** (about 20 words): "That is the brief. Full details, sources, and links are on the page. See you tomorrow morning."
